## Host compatibility profile

- **Required host behavior:** Load Marquee; expose the user-authorized CareerHub plan or its supplied contents; optionally retrieve relevant authorized graph or Work IQ context; support a complete draft; and reveal destination edit capability separately from submission authority.
- **Capability hypothesis:** Cowork should support a conversational, transfer-ready plan even without CareerHub integration. CareerHub inspection, direct editing, Work IQ retrieval, and durable graph mutation are independent, unverified capabilities; none is required to claim success for a high-quality user-managed draft.
- **Focused capability checks:** Confirm Skill invocation; inspect a privacy-safe sample plan; test authorized graph and Work IQ retrieval separately with minimal scope; determine whether a disposable CareerHub field can be edited and independently observed. Never probe submission using a real plan.
- **Another host:** Identify its product and repeat only the checks needed for the intended completion level. Compare source visibility, context retrieval, editing, review controls, fallback, and effect reporting; record the predicted adaptation before the complete journey.

## Success decision

Determine whether a participant can complete a credible CareerHub Development Plan with less reconstruction and stronger career insight while retaining control. Authority, factual accuracy, privacy, and effect truth must pass; experience behaviors must average at least 2.

## Test experience

A beta participant invokes Marquee with their real CareerHub Development Plan on the production AI surface. Marquee establishes or uses the graph, agrees on involvement, reviews only authorized context, advises, produces the plan, retains approved learning, and reports actual effects. The participant submits independently unless exact submission is separately authorized.

## Evidence and stop conditions

The participant reviews private work results locally; retain only privacy-minimized ratings, counts, decisions, capability observations, and non-identifying excerpts. Independently inspect authorized durable effects. Stop for privacy exposure, unsupported claims, unauthorized submission, identity confusion, destructive change, or false completion reporting.
