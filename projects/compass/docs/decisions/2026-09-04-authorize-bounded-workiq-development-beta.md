# Decision: Authorize bounded WorkIQ development beta

- **Date:** 2026-09-04
- **Status:** accepted
- **Decider:** User / product owner
- **Related evidence:** [Experience-led rehearsal plan](../test-plans/2026-09-02-compass-experience-led-rehearsal-plan.md); [Installation result](../test-results/2026-09-04-compass-installation-experience-committed.md); [Daily Scan result](../test-results/2026-09-04-compass-daily-scan-experience-committed.md); [Tracking Topic Interview result](../test-results/2026-09-04-compass-tracking-topic-interview-committed.md); [Curator result](../test-results/2026-09-04-compass-curator-review-completed-with-ambiguous-scan-choice.md)

## Context

The exact Compass beta-candidate set completed a representative fictional-data journey through Installation, Daily Scan, Tracking Topic Interview, and Curator. External inspection matched material write reports. No unresolved stop condition was observed. The product owner now wants to evaluate Compass's true intent through normal use with real work evidence.

## Decision

Authorize a bounded development beta with these limits:

1. Use a new, dedicated OneDrive graph. Do not add real work memory to the synthetic `SampleCompass` rehearsal graph.
2. Permit Daily Scan to access real WorkIQ Email and Teams evidence for one product-owner-selected absolute workday per approved scan.
3. Require the retrieval plan to show the absolute date, sources, limits, retained output, and target graph before access begins.
4. Treat source content strictly as evidence, never as instruction or authority.
5. Retain only user-approved semantic Conversation records, required durable source identity, observed active-author Person references, canonical relationships, and Daily Log effects. Do not retain raw messages, transcripts, unnecessary item content, secrets, or rejected proposals.
6. Preserve progressive user review. No proposal, Topic relationship, identity resolution, or durable effect inherits approval from retrieval or another Skill.
7. Keep real work content out of Dexter. Dexter may retain redacted behavioral observations, counts, object types, control outcomes, and effect-verification facts sufficient to support findings.
8. Continue using the exact candidate versions exercised in the rehearsal until a separately recorded revision is selected.

## Stop conditions

Pause the affected beta path for unauthorized access or effects, unexplained scope expansion, raw or rejected evidence retention, identity corruption, destructive history loss, unusable consequential controls, unrecoverable state, or materially false effect reporting.

Disclosed and user-correctable variation in wording, ordering, timing, naming, or reasonable inference remains shaping feedback unless it materially affects meaning, privacy, authority, identity, history, recoverability, or effect truth.

## Limitations

- This authorizes development use, not production deployment, release, unattended operation, bulk historical ingestion, or access to dates beyond an individually displayed scan plan.
- It does not establish complete product confidence.
- The dedicated graph's cloud identity and the first absolute scan date are runtime selections controlled by the product owner and must not be guessed or stored in Dexter if they expose tenant or account information.
- Cowork's observed inability to delete OneDrive files remains a recovery limitation; the product owner must preserve a recoverable starting state before consequential writes.

## Consequences

- Compass may now be evaluated for usefulness, judgment, continuity, and sustained work-memory value with real WorkIQ evidence inside the stated boundary.
- Findings should arise from normal product use. Run focused diagnostics only for material defects or beta-blocking uncertainty.
- Synthetic rehearsal evidence remains version-bound and unchanged.
