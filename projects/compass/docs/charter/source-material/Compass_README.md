# Quick Start Guide

## Start with your work. Build your graph. Show your progress.

This project helps you turn everyday work into a living knowledge graph.

It starts with the basics: **Tracking Topics, People, Conversations, and CSPs**. From there, it refines what you do into progress tracking, durable context, customer success planning, review preparation, and reusable memory for future AI experiences.

The experience introduces [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) as a place to capture and associate knowledge through the refinement of your work activity:

> Conversations become evidence. People become context. Tracking Topics become progress. The graph becomes memory.

---

## The Contoso Example

Contoso is the fictional company used to explain Compass. An experienced technical advisor at Contoso works closely with a customer team while balancing immediate delivery with longer-term advisory work. Their days produce changing Tracking Topics, decisions, relationships, and follow-ups across Microsoft 365.

Compass helps the advisor turn approved evidence from that work into a durable, user-controlled knowledge graph. AI helps recover context and maintain continuity, while the advisor retains judgment and authority over what becomes trusted knowledge. Contoso, its people, and its customers are fictional and do not represent a real organization or person.

## What You Will Build

You will build two connected parts that make your knowledge durable, traceable, and useful.

### 1. Knowledge Graph Scaffolding

The foundation is a folder structure hosted in OneDrive and opened as an Obsidian vault. It contains Markdown files that hold your information and construct your knowledge graph. A Conversation may align to one Tracking Topic, and a Tracking Topic may align to one CSP. A Conversation without a Tracking Topic appears in the Parking Lot.

Because the graph is made of Markdown files, you can inspect, correct, and reuse the knowledge while OneDrive keeps it available across your connected experiences.

### 2. Cowork Knowledge Management Skills

You will integrate a series of skills with Cowork to manage and maintain the knowledge in the graph:

1. **Installation:** Cowork prepares or validates the OneDrive-hosted graph and confirms that it is usable in Obsidian.
2. **Daily Scan:** Cowork reviews your email and Teams activity for one selected local calendar day, then proposes one Conversation per distinct thread or chat for your approval. An approved Conversation can remain in the Parking Lot or continue to Tracking Topic review.
3. **Tracking Topic conversation:** You can create, discuss, update, connect, or archive Tracking Topics through natural conversation. This skill owns every Topic action and Conversation-to-Topic connection. When a new Topic fits a displayed Conversation, Cowork proposes the Topic and link together for one confirmation.
4. **Lifecycle management:** Cowork checks graph health, performs only safe and uniquely determined repairs, and helps recover interrupted writes while preserving history and reporting what changed.

Compass proposes; you confirm, edit, or initiate. Valid direct edits remain authoritative, and every approved workflow uses the same validation and persistence rules before changing the graph.

This foundation helps you see what changed, why it changed, who was involved, and what needs attention next. The result is not a pile of AI-generated notes. It is a traceable graph you can inspect, correct, and reuse.
