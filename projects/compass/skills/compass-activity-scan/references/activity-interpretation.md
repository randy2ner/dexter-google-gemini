# Customer Activity Interpretation

An Activity is one coherent unit of meaningful customer work, not an Email thread, Teams chat, meeting, or message container. Customer work is materially attributable to an identifiable customer relationship, outcome, commitment, or need. Internal preparation or coordination qualifies only when that connection is concrete.

Several source items may support one Activity, and one source may support several Activities. State what happened, why it matters to the customer, participating People, and one Effort or Parking Lot disposition. Include progress, decisions, risks, commitments, outcomes, and next steps only when supported. Preserve minimized provenance and distinguish source facts from interpretation.

Set `lastActivityAt` to the newest reliable timestamp among qualifying customer-work items. Never substitute container start or modification time, retrieval time, file time, Daily Log time, or a recent unrelated item. Follow available continuation. If item timestamps or coverage are incomplete, conflicting, future-dated, or timezone-ambiguous, leave recency uncertain.

Correlate to an existing Activity only when the coherent customer work and graph context support continuity. Source identity, title, keywords, or participants alone are insufficient. Ask one focused question or leave the proposal unresolved when continuity, identity, customer relevance, or Effort placement remains materially ambiguous.
