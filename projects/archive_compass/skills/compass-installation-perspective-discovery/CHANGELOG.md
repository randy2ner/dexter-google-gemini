# Changelog

## 0.1.2-experimental — 2026-09-01

### Changed

- Candidate review now uses short progressive turns with no more than three primary choices.
- Technical review metadata appears only on request.
- Questions and `Pause`, `Stop`, and `Cancel` use ordinary typed conversation.
- A visibly disclosed UTC fallback replaces blocking when Cowork exposes no IANA timezone.
- Added a fixed, zero-retrieval synthetic interface preview for PD-UI-001.

### Unchanged

- Email and Teams scope, seven-day window, inspection limits, minimization, retrieval authorization, handoff authority, and no-external-change contract.

## 0.1.1-experimental — 2026-09-01

### Changed

- Candidate review now begins with a plain-language explanation and natural question.
- User-visible choices are `Yes`, `Change the wording`, `Make it more general`, and `Leave this out`.
- Technical candidate metadata remains secondary and is explained as review reference information.
- The Skill explicitly welcomes questions, pausing, and correction before a decision.

### Unchanged

- Read-only retrieval boundary, sources, date window, inspection limits, minimization, authority, handoff, terminal accounting, and no-external-change contract.

## 0.1.0-experimental — 2026-09-01

### Added

- Initial bounded Perspective Discovery source.
