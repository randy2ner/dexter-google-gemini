# Conditional Access Policy

## Objective

Enforce Zero Trust principles by controlling access based on risk, user context, and device compliance.

## Key Actions

- Centralize access control through Microsoft Entra Conditional Access for consistent enforcement across applications and resources.
- Implement risk-based policies, including stronger authentication for high-risk sign-ins and blocking legacy authentication.
- Use report-only mode before enforcement to minimize disruption.
- Maintain emergency access accounts with strict monitoring and governance.

## Success Criteria

- Critical applications are covered by Conditional Access policies.
- Adaptive policy enforcement reduces unauthorized or risky sign-ins.
- Access-decision telemetry supports validation of policy effectiveness and compliance.

## Stakeholders

- Customer authentication services
- Customer identity and access management
