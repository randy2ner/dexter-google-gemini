## Current Product Summary: Projection

- **What the product does:** Compass is a portable set of Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork Skills that helps an individual maintain a durable work-memory graph using Work IQ evidence and user-controlled Markdown/YAML in OneDrive and Obsidian.
- **What goes wrong today:** Work knowledge is maintained manually in OneNote and reconstructed from fragmented email, chat, notes, and memory.
- **Who it serves:** An individual Microsoft 365 Copilot user managing CSPs, Tracking Topics, people, Conversations, and evolving work context.
- **What done enough for the first release means:** Installation, Daily Scan, Tracking Topic Conversation, and Lifecycle Management satisfy the acceptance criteria in the accepted product spec without unapproved authoritative changes, historical loss, or routine micromanagement.

## Current Approved Scope: Projection

### In Scope For The First Release

- Portable Cowork Skills with no standalone Compass application.
- OneDrive-hosted Markdown/YAML graph usable in Obsidian.
- Work IQ evidence presented as proposals until user confirmation.
- Consolidated Yes/No review with evidence and context-sensitive rejected-item follow-up.
- Daily Scan reviews one selected local calendar day and proposes one Conversation per source-proven email thread or Teams chat for explicit review.
- Conversation summaries link to People and zero or one Tracking Topic; absence of `trackingTopicId` projects the Conversation into Parking Lot.
- Tracking Topic Conversation owns all Topic activity and Conversation-to-Topic links, including one-confirmation new-Topic-plus-link proposals.
- Zero-or-one alignment from Conversation to Tracking Topic and from Tracking Topic to CSP.
- CSP deactivation and Tracking Topic archiving that preserve history.
- Workflow-owned writes through one Shared Graph Capability, with Lifecycle Management responsible for health checks, deterministic repair, recovery, and reporting.
- Optimistic concurrency and journaled, recoverable multi-file changes that stop on ambiguous reconciliation.
- Low-interruption delegation using approved context, preferences, and sensible defaults.

### Explicitly Out Of Scope For The First Release

- Automatic migration of the existing OneNote corpus.
- Raw transcript storage by default.
- Automatic promotion of Work IQ findings into authoritative knowledge.
- Destructive deletion as normal lifecycle behavior.
- Autonomous changes to user-authored guidance.
- Product implementation, deployment, and changes that depart from the accepted technical specification without review.

Basis: Decisions PS-001 through PS-013 and accepted artifacts `docs/product-spec.md`, `docs/technical-spec.md`, and `docs/adr/0002-decouple-conversation-identity-from-work-iq.md`.

- **Primary user:** Owns the graph, approves proposed knowledge, and supplies consequential business decisions.
- **Compass:** Retrieves evidence, proposes connections, performs authorized routine work, and preserves user authority.
- **Work IQ:** Provides authorized Microsoft 365 evidence; it is not an authority over the graph.
